QCan I transfer my ticket to someone else?
AYes. Tickets are non-refundable but transferable within the same company. Just inform us 5 working days before the workshop so we can update your attendee information.
QIs there any translation service?
AThe workshop will be conducted entirely in English, and no translation will be provided.
QWhat if I am running late?
APlease inform us in the WhatsApp group. Late comers will need to self-arrange their transportation to the specific venue.
QCan I arrange my own transportation to each venue?
ANo. All participants must follow our schedule and travel on our bus for the whole trip, except for those who are late for registration.
